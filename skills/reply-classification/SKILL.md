# Reply Classification

Choose one of `inquiry`, `quotation_request`, `catalog_request`, `sample_request`,
`meeting_request`, `objection`, `not_interested`, `unsubscribe`, `out_of_office`,
or `unclear`.  Record only explicit buyer signals and list missing SKU, quantity,
destination, deadline, and commercial terms.  Unsubscribe immediately blocks
future outreach.  All recommended replies and quotations require human review.
