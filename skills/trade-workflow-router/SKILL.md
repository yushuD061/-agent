# Trade Workflow Router

Use the internal trade workbench for product loading, compliant discovery, list
enrichment, company research, scoring, decision-maker clues, email drafts, reply
classification, follow-up plans, and quotation drafts.  Run only the selected
stage and its prerequisites.  Every result must contain evidence, risks,
missing inputs, and next required inputs.  Internal stages may continue when
safe; email approval, email queueing, and quotation approval are separate human
actions.  Never contact a prospect or publish a quotation automatically.
