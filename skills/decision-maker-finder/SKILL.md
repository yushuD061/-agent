# Decision Maker Finder

Search approved official pages for Owner, Founder, Purchasing, Sourcing,
Category, Procurement, and Buyer roles.  Always perform separate email and phone
searches.  Each candidate needs role, source URL, evidence, confidence,
`email_status` (`missing|invalid_format|format_valid|domain_match|api_verified`),
and phone status.  A role clue is not purchase intent.  Never send outreach.
