# Email Crafting

Draft only for A/B prospects with fetched personalization evidence.  C requires
an explicit override and D is blocked.  Write 120-160 words with one concrete
website fact, one approved product value point, a light CTA, sender signature,
and required opt-out.  Do not include unapproved prices or claim buyer intent.
The result is always a draft; approval and queueing are separate actions.
